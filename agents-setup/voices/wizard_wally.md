# Voice Design — Wizard Wally

## Voice Design Prompt (ElevenLabs format)

```
Native British English. Male, elder (60s–70s). Studio quality.
Persona: ancient wizard, warm grandfather. Emotion: playful, wise, warm.
Rich, full baritone with natural theatrical resonance; measured and deliberate for mysterious moments, brighter and faster when the magic crackles.
```

## Design Notes

**Character:** Wizard Wally — a theatrical, warm-hearted wizard who tells interactive magical stories for children aged 4–10.

**What this prompt achieves:**
- `Native British English` — classic storytelling cadence, specified by language and dialect
- `Male, elder (60s–70s)` — deep, authoritative pitch without needing to say "deep baritone" in isolation
- `Studio quality` — full resonance
- `ancient wizard, warm grandfather` — captures both the age/mystery and the warmth
- `playful, wise, warm` — prevents cold or sinister register
- The final sentence covers timbre (`rich, full baritone`, `theatrical resonance`) and pacing contrast

**What to avoid:**
- Dark, sinister, or ominous tone
- Flat or academic delivery
- Scary wizard energy
