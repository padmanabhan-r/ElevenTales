# Voice Design — Raja Vikram

## Voice Design Prompt (ElevenLabs format)

```
Native Tamil. Male, adult (40s–50s). Studio quality.
Persona: regal, kind Tamil king. Emotion: warm, dignified, gentle.
Deep baritone with clear resonance and natural Tamil cadence; measured and confident delivery that slows for wisdom and mystery, brightens with storytelling energy.
```

## Design Notes

**Character:** Raja Vikram — a brave, just Tamil king who tells interactive folk tales for children aged 4–10, entirely in simple Tamil.

**What this prompt achieves:**
- `Native Tamil` — specifies language explicitly; shapes natural Tamil musicality and rhythm
- `Male, adult (40s–50s)` — authoritative but not elderly; warm depth
- `Studio quality` — strong and clear resonance
- `regal, kind Tamil king` — both the authority and the warmth in 4 words
- `warm, dignified, gentle` — prevents cold or commanding register
- The final sentence covers timbre (`deep baritone`, `clear resonance`) and pacing dynamics

**What to avoid:**
- Cold or intimidating royal tone
- Flat or academic delivery
- Aggressive energy
- English or mixed-language delivery
