# Voice Design — Fairy Flora

## Voice Design Prompt (ElevenLabs format)

```
Native British English. Female, young adult (20s). Studio quality.
Persona: whimsical fairy storyteller. Emotion: warm, delighted, magical.
Light soprano voice with an airy, breathy quality and natural musical lilt; pacing varies from slow hushed near-whispers at magical reveals to bright and quick with excitement.
```

## Design Notes

**Character:** Fairy Flora — a kind, joyful fairy from the Enchanted Garden who tells interactive stories for children aged 4–10.

**What this prompt achieves:**
- `Native British English` — specifies language and dialect explicitly, shapes cadence without using the word "accent"
- `Female, young adult (20s)` — bright, clear pitch range without sounding childish
- `Studio quality` — clean, full voice
- `whimsical fairy storyteller` — persona in 3 words
- `warm, delighted, magical` — emotional register
- The final sentence describes timbre (`airy, breathy`), musicality (`lilt`), and pacing dynamics

**What to avoid:**
- Flat or measured delivery
- Too deep or mature
- Any harshness or edge
