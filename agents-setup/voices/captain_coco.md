# Voice Design — Captain Coco

## Voice Design Prompt (ElevenLabs format)

```
Native English. Female, adult (30s). Studio quality.
Persona: bold, warm-hearted pirate captain. Emotion: energetic, brave, joyful.
Clear mid-range voice with bold, punchy delivery and natural forward momentum; slows to wonder at discoveries, speeds up with the thrill of adventure.
```

## Design Notes

**Character:** Captain Coco — a bold, warm-hearted pirate captain who tells interactive adventure stories for children aged 4–10.

**What this prompt achieves:**
- `Native English` — clear, carrying voice without regional restriction
- `Female, adult (30s)` — strong, capable pitch range
- `Studio quality` — crisp and clear
- `bold, warm-hearted pirate captain` — captures the duality (bold exterior, warm heart)
- `energetic, brave, joyful` — prevents aggressive or scary energy
- The final sentence describes delivery style (`punchy`, `forward momentum`) and pacing dynamics

**What to avoid:**
- Aggressive or scary pirate energy
- Too gravelly or dark
- Monotone or flat
- Over-the-top parody
